<h1><img src="/docs/assets/icons/zeeguu.svg" style="width:40px; margin-right: 0.5em"/>Zeeguu</h1>

Zeeguu is a free, open-source platform for learning languages by reading and listening to material you care about, at a level that fits you. It finds articles that match each learner's interests, supports reading with in-context translation and pronunciation, and turns the words a learner looked up into personalized exercises and daily audio lessons.

I started Zeeguu in 2016 and have led it since, together with [Tiago Ribeiro](https://github.com/tfnribeiro) and [many students over the years](https://zeeguu.org/about#contributors).
